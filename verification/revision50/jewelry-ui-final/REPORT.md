# Pre-potion Revision 50 jewelry visual QA

## Scope

Inspected all 11 PNGs in this directory with `view_image`, generated by the three passing `revision50_jewelry_capture` tests reported by the coordinating build owner. These are direct Run/Painter projections, not a production-admitted R50 game, SDL event-loop playtest, or final R50 UI acceptance. Approved concise jewelry copy and no-use-identification changes were still forthcoming. No Cargo command or production source change was made during inspection.

## Findings requiring follow-up

1. `statuses-full.png` and `statuses-half.png`: Discord renders as “Discord: hostil”. Its remaining-turn number is completely truncated. `render.rs::condition_meter` truncates the concatenated name and duration to the fixed meter width. Expected: a compact readable label retaining the remaining turns, with full hostility meaning accessible through details/hover. Rooted's 4→2 and both full→half bars are legible.
2. Already-worn rings and the amulet show both Wear and Take off. This is visible in the item captures. Expected: worn jewelry should not offer a redundant Wear action. Reported to the production owner before edits.
3. Ring detail still says “Ring · Ring finger”, and worn lists use `[ring]`/`[amulet]`. These are legacy slot-like labels alongside the no-slot-limit header, worth reconciling in the approved concise-copy pass. Canonical names themselves are correct.

## Successful observations

- `light-duplicates.png`: two worn Light rings remain separate entries; text correctly says each adds +10 illumination.
- `vampirism.png`, `ice-immunity.png`: canonical names and authored effect descriptions are legible and contained within the detail column.
- `long-ring-top.png`, `long-ring-bottom.png`: real multi-grant copy wraps and scrolls; bottom actions remain reachable. Partial lines at the scroll viewport edges are intentional clipping within the scroll region, not spillover. No text paints across panel boundaries.
- `amulet-full.png`, `amulet-spent-appropriate.png`: item energy changes from 50/50 to 25/50, activation cost from 25 to 15, and No cooldown remains visible. Appropriate and category capacities also refresh.
- `amulet-target-preview.png`: the straight ray appears along the expected row. `amulet-cancel-restored.png`: ray is removed and the baseline scene restored. The passing test checks exact baseline pixel restoration and unchanged Run serialization for three Apply/cancel projection cycles. It does not execute an activation or drive the main SDL cancellation event path.
- `statuses-full.png`, `statuses-half.png`: meter bars visibly change from full to half without disturbing the map/toolbar layout.

## Coverage limit

The test asserts data for Discord, Rooted and Corroded, but the screenshots show only the first two meters. The existing HUD renders two at a time and main.rs provides scrolling; page two was not captured here, so Corroded pixel presentation and status paging are not visually verified by this set. No general hidden-information or platform correctness claim is made.

Follow-up: retain these as pre-potion evidence; regenerate captures after final concise copy/identification and any targeted fixes, then inspect those actual pixels before calling the UI final.
