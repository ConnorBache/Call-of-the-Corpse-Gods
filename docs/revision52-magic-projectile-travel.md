# Magic lights and physical projectile travel

## Designer rule

Magic lights tiles. Physical projectiles move their actual glyph. Ability procs and areas retain exact tile illumination; ordinary movement and melee remain quiet. This amendment applies to R52 receipts, including allied and hostile casters. Older revision presentation retains its historical receipt path.

`AnimationCue.delivery` explicitly separates Local, Magic and Projectile. Traveling Lightning, Firebolt, Poison and Acid amulets, witch and bloodmancer spells, Hornlight, Moonbeam and Water Jet use Magic. Physical arrows, thrown equipment, thrown potion bottles and Distant Spit use Projectile and preserve their actual admitted glyph. Areas, heals, statuses and symbolic links remain Local tile illumination; their endpoints are never turned into synthetic paths.

## Brogue source and deliberate timing adjustment

Pinned Brogue CE source: `22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22`.

- [Flame/arrow definitions](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/blob/22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22/src/variants/GlobalsBrogue.c#L58-L89): flame has no projectile glyph; its fire-colored background lights the underlying tile. Arrow has G_WEAPON, gray foreground and no background light.
- [Creature collision and fire](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/blob/22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22/src/brogue/Items.c#L4349-L4684): ordinary fire collision skips submerged creatures and stops at the first non-submerged creature, even if killed or immune. It does not pierce ordinary creatures.
- [Terrain combustion](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/blob/22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22/src/brogue/Time.c#L1181-L1242) happens before [the remaining obstruction check](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/blob/22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22/src/brogue/Items.c#L5050-L5053). A burned-open tree/door permits continuation; a remaining solid/opaque layer stops it.
- [Travel rendering](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/blob/22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22/src/brogue/Items.c#L4932-L4998) requests 16 ms per visible cell for both flame and centaur arrow, and [retracts the light tail](https://github.com/tmewett/BrogueCE/blob/22c64f58f92509364525800ddbde21b724911c22/src/brogue/Items.c#L5103-L5142). Centaur uses BOLT_DISTANCE_ATTACK through monsterCastSpell/zap, not the separate 25 ms thrown-item loop. Brogue's visual tail length `5 * magnitude` is not flight range.

For the requested readability, CotCG deliberately uses 32 ms per admitted magic cell and 40 ms per physical cell. It does not claim exact Brogue timing. Magic has a short 128 ms fading/retracting light tail; physical shots show one glyph with clean previous cells. The route is never compressed into a fixed total duration. Impact/health/death stages are scheduled after the corresponding route reaches its destination. Simultaneous action groups retain the existing bounded overlap. Reduced/Instant mode presents static endpoint/area lighting.

## Firebolt mechanics and presentation

The new Firebolt travel skips underwater creatures and ignites each actually entered terrain cell before testing the remaining blocking layers. It stops on the first ordinary creature regardless of damage outcome, including a phased creature standing inside an otherwise blocking cell. CotCG authored damage, costs, reserves, range convention, statuses and immunity rules remain unchanged. No Brogue reflection system or new splash attack is added. The portal lottery and cycle bounds remain CotCG's existing spatial-link contract; unchosen exits are not ignited.

`fireboltTravelFrom` is a Firebolt-only command boundary, not a global revision bump. Old recorded prefixes use the original trace-then-burn behavior. Newly admitted live commands use the new travel. Save/load, replay restart/jump and temporal restores preserve the boundary outside authoritative snapshot hashes. Markerless R52 incremental journals replay their entire durable tail with legacy Firebolt before adopting the new policy at the final command boundary. Their first resumed save writes a fresh checkpoint before any current-policy append; explicit integer and null policies are preserved.

The world still resolves synchronously before visual playback. A detached pre-cast observation masks only already-observed terrain/fire layers until the visible head reaches them. A recoverable physical item is likewise withheld from its exact observed destination until arrival, using the launched item ID rather than a glyph guess; existing ground items and scattered destinations remain current. Canonical View, actor state, inspection knowledge, save data and gameplay are never rolled backward to render an animation. Hidden terrain is not captured or inferred, and discontinuities are never interpolated. Animations start at their first painted frame after dispatch so expensive simulation cannot consume their entire duration before any frame is shown. Explicit new commands may interrupt presentation; no cosmetic timer controls gameplay.

## Complexity

Let E be queued receipts (cap 4096), C total admitted route cells, V floor cells, A actors, P touched raster pixels, B observed snapshot bytes, and Q the existing visibility-query cost including cold lighting/portal caches.

Recording explicit delivery and glyph metadata adds O(1 + glyph bytes) work. Existing exact-cell filtering remains O(C * Q) time/O(C) detached storage. Scheduling sorts O(E log E) and builds per-action travel durations in O(E log E), O(E) scratch. Each frame projects physical heads in O(1) per route and magic in O(C) across the exact route, retaining at most four tail cells per route for the current constants. Existing visible-cell lookup/merge, native font cold-cache rasterization and full/tile damage projection costs still apply. Per-cell timing adds no simulation work or RNG calls.

Firebolt simulation uses at most 2V committed integer ray steps with portal preflight and cycle guard. With A actors, alias query cost H and live link query cost G, traversal adds O(V*(A*H+G)) plus the unchanged pure ray-plan cost. One freeze-mask and one FireAdapter initialization/resolution occur per cast, not per tile; the existing fire closure retains O(Bursts * radius² * LOS + V * alpha(V)), radius at most 9. Extra path, visited-group and fire buffers are O(V). Preview adds a detached floor clone and O(V * visible freezers) known burn mask on top of existing observation/ray costs. Actor occupancy, spatial aliases, flammable layer handling and propagation are not constant-time assumptions. Replay boundary admission/checking is O(1); serialization adds one optional integer field, while existing save/hash cloning retains its original full-state costs. Journal migration adds O(1) marker checks to the existing checkpoint JSON parse and one O(1) adoption after the existing bounded tail replay. The migration flag uses O(1) extra memory; the first resumed save pays the existing full-checkpoint serialization, framing and atomic write cost rather than a receipt append. No additional replay or per-frame work is introduced.

See [the detailed staged-rendering bounds](revision52-firebolt-staging.md) for cold reconstruction and glyph-overhang damage. Staged Firebolt capture conditionally observes the pre-cast world, paying the existing O(observation) cost and O(B) detached memory. Per-frame preparation scans exact observed route cells; changed withheld terrain rebuilds detached paint cells/snapshot and conservatively invalidates the map. Native rerasterization costs O(P) plus font/glyph cache work, and may be a full-map paint at each arrival rather than O(changed cells). Actor layers and UI queries use the unchanged canonical View. This is a correctness-first bounded path, not a measured performance improvement.
