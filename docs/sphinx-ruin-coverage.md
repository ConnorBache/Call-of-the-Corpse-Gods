## Complexity

Let A be current-floor actor count, L ray length, V floor cells and D the transitive cost of damage/death hooks. Sphinx uses stable-ID scans and LOS: O(A + L) excluding presentation visibility/cache rebuilds and actor-vector copy-on-write. Ruin Pulse copies O(A) IDs, then performs O(A) stable lookups and LOS/damage, hence O(A * (A + L + D)) worst-case time and O(A) scratch, plus visibility O(V)-scale cache rebuilds when required. Damage may transfer corpses/items to deeper floors and create floors; these dependent costs are not constant. The world aura refresh clears markers and expires walls before an ordered O(A² * L) hostile-body LOS scan, with O(A) body snapshot scratch. Offscreen refresh adds one actor lookup per non-consuming specialist action. These are analytical bounds, not speed measurements or a claim of exhaustive whole-game parity.
