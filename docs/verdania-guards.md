# Guarded Verdania taps

Newly populated revision52 Verdania floors place the tap at the farthest **eligible reachable** dry tile, measured by ordinary eight-direction shortest path from the up stair (diagonal wall restrictions and grates respected). All legal adjacent approach cells must be safe dry spawn positions and must match the member count of a single authored ground encounter selected from the roster two displayed layers deeper. The whole pack is sleeping. It is an extra guard encounter, not a replacement for an ordinary pack. Creature stats remain the fixed per-species stats.

For twenty-floor games, displayed depth +2 is converted back to content depth; the final-layer roster is capped at20 displayed/40 content. Godbeasts and awake-only recipes are excluded. No terrain is carved or removed. Existing actors, machines, fixtures and protected areas are respected. If no valid cut exists, complete-floor generation retries (existing bound8), then returns explicit failure rather than silently leaving an unguarded tap.

“Hardest” here means greatest walking distance among placements that meet the guard guarantee, not an unprovable optimal challenge for every build. The guards fill every immediate normal-movement approach to the tap, so alternate ordinary paths cannot avoid them; teleportation, flight across chasms, phasing, displacement, and other special abilities are not prohibited. After guards die, the tap remains normally reachable. Already-generated/saved floors are not retrofitted. No animation changes in this module.

Guard encounter records use `verdania-guard`, so their normal heart rewards are additional to (not deducted from or mislabeled as) the ordinary floor blessing budget. The separate category also prevents ordinary-horde biome relocation from moving guards away. A final post-decoration check rejects any changed terrain or displacement that opens a normal movement approach.

Validation: deterministic full population sweep exercised six biome layouts at underlying content depths5,25,35,37 (24 cases), forcing the Verdania event to cover all geometries rather than depending on its shuffled identity. All24 accepted within the eight-attempt bound;13 total complete-floor retries, maximum3 in one case. These retry counts include all existing geometry/population failure causes, not just tap selection. Normal actors, machine reservations, loot and late decorators were included. This is finite regression evidence, not a mathematical guarantee that every seed can be generated. Current authored boss placement ends at displayed18; displayed19/20 roster capping is additionally checked directly.
