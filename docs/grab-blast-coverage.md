# Native held movement, River Drake and Bombardier

`monster_grab` ports Maulchild's repeated non-damaging hold, Saidribee Guardian and River Drake's ordinary grab, Kelpie's deep-water-only longer hold, holder expiration and invalid-holder cleanup, held movement attempts and forced-movement bypass. Water jet preserves authored range, cost, hit check, forced knockback and source forcedMove removal. The engine calls invalid-grab cleanup at the post-command boundary. Actor clocks validate/decrement grabs after venom and before shared beast cooldowns. Hidden floor movement now invokes the same full enemyMoved terrain hook, preserving submersion rather than clearing it unconditionally.

27 raw grab/movement/jet fixtures and three hidden-floor cases pass strict complete-state/RNG/event comparison, including a jet that forces the player down a chasm. All seven full command/history/event traces also pass.

`monster_bombardier` spends all currently available Energy, then invokes the existing radius-five explosion with ignition-before-damage ordering and source attribution. Ten raw cases pass, including tree removal, chained explosive gas, shared Half Here bodies and a lethal victim. One hidden-floor case and all four full command/history/event traces also pass. Explosion immunity remains separate from fire immunity. Three additional full-state/event fixtures verify venom cleanup after cross-floor corpse transfer, born-from-Birther disappearance and terminal-floor discard.

## Complexity

For A current-floor actors, F affected floors, V cells/floor and L ray length, invalid-grab cleanup currently uses stable-ID lookups per actor: O(A²) worst-case time, O(A) ID scratch, plus any actor-vector COW detachment. Single actor grab clocks cost O(A). Holding and presentation add visibility/log helper costs. Water jet adds projectile trace O(L*A), combat resolution and engine movement; forced player descent can invoke suffix generation, catch-up and full fall placement, so it is not bounded by one local tile. Bombardier itself performs constant-size selection and one resource update; the radius-five native explosion includes ordered ignition, LOS, actor damage, gas-reaction closure, terrain changes and dependent death/lifecycle costs. Those environment costs are intentionally not described as O(1). These bounds are analytical, without a speed or whole-game-parity claim.
