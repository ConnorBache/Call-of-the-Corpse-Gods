# Quiet UI and gameplay follow-up (R51)

Designer requests: keep the observed monster profiles on the left; highlighting a monster or its profile highlights the other. Reduce corpse X markers, retain terrain beneath the player, right-align divine metadata without artificial clipping, hide unused reserves, use native occupied-window pixels automatically, remove unsolicited instructional text, keep only Pack / Bestiary / Menu evenly distributed on the bottom, and shorten conditions to names.

The UI implementation is presentation-only. New runs use simulation revision 51 for new rounding, fungus-feast and unique Composite bow rules. Existing saves retain their recorded revision; stored reserve capacities remain unchanged. The requested restoration of four energy costs and immediate-benefit upkeep correction are documented in revision50-ability-energy.md; serialized paid-turn conditions prevent duplicate charges. These are explicit gameplay amendments, not a claim of identical R50 combat behavior. Hover resolves only detached observed cells and creature rows, so it cannot reveal hidden actors. Both old and new highlight footprints are invalidated for full, retained and tile rendering. Toolbar hit regions and labels share the same equal-third geometry at all responsive widths; removed actions retain their keyboard controls.

Ability metadata uses measured actual state width and all remaining line space, aligns cost to the right edge, and ellipsizes only when the complete line cannot fit. An authored fixed list of energy-consuming R50 abilities suppresses reserve values for free, passive, health-, reaction-, blood- and nightmare-paid abilities. Energy-using abilities retain reserves in temporarily free return/removal modes. Stored reserves remain untouched.

The corpse cross now has a square footprint, 2.8 logical pixels between endpoints at a 15-pixel-wide tile, plus a narrow outline. The ordinary ring diameter is roughly 4.8 logical pixels. Native raster regression bounds the resulting cross within twice that ring footprint. Terrain/light/material and custom appearance backgrounds remain under the player glyph, without the hardcoded near-black backing. Existing self-light-ring exclusion remains unchanged.

Removed added hints, empty-condition text, sensed/distance lines, duplicate creature counts, low-health text, bottom provenance, and passive hover scene narration. Preserved authored descriptions, resources, conditions/durations, action names and actionable error/targeting messages. Full ability and creature descriptions remain available through inspection.

## Additional requested changes

Five effective bonus stats sit below the resource bars: Damage, HP, DR, Accuracy and Dodge. Damage replaces internal Offense in nominal player melee-bonus units; HP replaces internal Defense at six HP per point, separately from total health in the bar. Stat copies and contextual creature damage use the conversions documented in revision51-stat-display-units.md. Internal serialized names and combat arithmetic remain compatible. Vampire uses uppercase V unless the user explicitly customized its glyph.

Bestiary discovery and unread flags persist alongside the selected player profile, independently of run saves. Only directly observed apparent species unlock pages; sensing and hallucinations do not reveal hidden identities. Unknown pages keep the book background and question mark. New discovery inserts the purple Chronicle message, and unread entries show a yellow exclamation mark. Atomic writes preserve the existing file on failure, and actionable storage errors appear once rather than silently losing persistence.

## Complexity

Let C be detached observed cells, A observed creatures, T metadata/condition text length, P native pixels, and M available display modes (at most 257 including a retained manual size). Pointer hit selection is O(C+A) time/O(1) memory; two-way map highlight resolution and old/new invalidation each scan C, O(C)/O(1), only over detached observations. No world actor lookup or simulation dispatch. Existing preview content wrapping remains worst-case O(T²) with O(T+A) temporary storage. Metadata fitting is O(T log T) worst-case measurement with O(T) temporary text; full fitting is a linear measurement pass. Energy-consumer classification is a fixed-ID comparison, O(ID bytes) bounded by catalog IDs. Toolbar is three O(1) primitives/hit regions plus font raster work. Cross rasterization is O(marker native area × 16 fixed samples), O(1) scratch. Removing player backing decreases pixel fills and does not add caches. Auto selection sorts M modes in O(M log M)/O(M); drawable/input mapping remains O(1), existing frame allocation/raster cost O(P). No new unbounded cache.
