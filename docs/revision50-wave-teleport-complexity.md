# Revision 50 wave and Teleportation

Teleportation targets another visible, same-plane creature within eight grid tiles. Its random destination is chosen using serialized gameplay RNG from the current floor, without an explored-state restriction, and checked against occupancy, standing habitat, passability and creature-specific environmental hazards. No destination consumes no RNG. Arrival uses the shared movement hooks, including Duel/Roots departure behavior. Cost and accounting remain in the shared amulet dispatcher.

Floods snapshots wave cells before adding shallow water, so its new water cannot stop its own propagation. Up to fifteen cells are reached; creatures take 20 direct damage using the activation-time player source and are pushed up to two steps through the forced-movement path. Front-to-back processing with actor-ID tie breaking keeps collision results independent of actor storage order. Solid terrain and closed doors block pushes, and pushes do not redirect through portals. Terrain replacement and twelve-turn expiration belong to the common control-amulet state. Quenching preserves persistent and gas flames. Each of the three lanes stops independently at existing deep or shallow water; a blocked lane does not truncate either neighboring lane. Preview and real activation use that same approved policy.

Inputs: V floor cells, A current-floor actors, P portal entries, B actor abilities/traits, H creature hazard query work, L visibility/light-source work. Teleport destination enumeration runs once per activation: O(V*(A+P+B+H)) time, O(V) scratch, in addition to visibility admission and existing arrival hooks. Visibility may build O(V) fields and scan sources; it is not assumed constant. Wave geometry is bounded to 15 cell checks; live target collection is O(A), sorting O(A log A), and up to 2A forced movements retain the engine's collision, portal, travel and environmental costs. Temporary-terrain storage inherits the common control-amulet ledger. Preview performs at most 15 visibility queries and includes visibility-cache construction/misses. No renderer or native-library work occurs here. Bounds are analytical estimates, not measured timings. Verify with focused native module tests plus the complete workspace suite after integration.

Teleportation admission excludes immutable decoys, Clay Soldiers, Ahvamitt and inert bodies, while retaining independent Mini-Me targets. Floods checks source-scoped Fated Duel protection before direct damage and push, without curing existing statuses or shielding the target’s ground from water. This adds the existing owner lookup and active-trait checks per affected actor; no new persistent state is introduced.

Final-choice regression sources exercise actual Floods amulet activation, its 30-energy cost, unchanged RNG, damage/push, deep/shallow-water independent stopping, twelve-turn restoration, diagonal geometry and atomic invalid-target rejection. These new cases await the integrated Cargo run.
