## Complexity

Let A be current-floor actors, V floor cells, B bulwarks, L LOS ray length, S actor metadata/condition bytes and Q visibility/stat query cost. Retreat builds an O(A) occupied-cell set, then at most8 traversal/LOS/hazard probes: O(A+8*(B+L+hazard lookup)+S), O(A+S) scratch. No retreat pathfield or RNG draw is introduced. Horn local choices examine at most25 cells with actor occupancy scans and LOS: O(25*(A+B+L)); stable candidate order determines its single random choice. Temporary wall expiration is O(A), plus existing ichor-opening queue cost, and can detach an O(V) floor COW block. Dryad target selection is O(A*(L+healing-suppression lookup)) with a single stable minimum; current implementation clones one selected Actor, O(S) scratch. Blood pull checks8 neighbor positions and occupancy O(8*A), with engine movement costs additive. Cast damage, derived stats, logging and visibility are existing subsystem costs and are not constant-time assumptions. Actor mutation may detach the entire per-floor actor vector. These are analytical bounds, not measured performance gains.
