# Native travel, falls and physical corpses

`travel.rs` ports stair/vertical/falling departure and arrival, nearest landing ties, catch-up sequencing, chase metadata and RNG, ally stair queues and return routes, safe ally arrival, finite-flight fall cohorts, collapsed-floor terrain/item/corpse transfer, movement terrain hooks and corpse placement. `corpse_placement.rs` preserves physical corpse-space occupancy separately from living actors and items, fungal-root severing, stable normalization order and placement metrics. These call native suffix generation when another floor is needed.

Source differential fixtures are generated from the frozen v48 modules during development; runtime is native Rust. `tests/travel.rs` compares complete states for18 travel/queue cases (including dynamically generated suffix11–20 and vertical ascending chasm landings),13 forced-fall cases,9 collapse cases and5 corpse-placement cases. Eight movement-hook fixtures verify yeti snow, Glim slime and forced movement, obstacle exclusions, and submersion/ichor/emergence. Additional collapse detection cases cover magic-sensitive and stale detected-item memory. `tests/travel_traces.rs` passes11 complete dispatch traces including explicit/automatic stairs, ordinary fall, finite-flight expiry, pit victory, haste, ally arrival and hunter pursuit; every state field, event, reconstructed history opening and pending-haste state is checked. `tests/death_transfer_traces.rs` additionally passes three full dispatch/history/event traces for a flying victim whose corpse and heart fall to a deeper floor, a born-from-Birther victim that vanishes, and terminal-floor corpse/heart discard. These preserve post-hit mutation of the relocated dead actor. Offscreen hunter/ally transfer tests belong to the environment suite.

Shared-health Half Here forced-fall/collapse endpoints now pass seven dedicated complete-state fixtures: endpoint-only, water, both bodies, lethal owner, and endpoint/both-body collapse and offscreen stale-endpoint dismissal. Normal travel dismisses the player clone according to source. Runtime admission is command-sensitive: standing over a chasm is not blanket permission to skip scheduling a fall. These tests do not establish every creature AI or interaction and do not establish platform launch or speedup.

## Complexity

Let D<=40 floors, V<=3600 cells/floor, A total actors, F queued followers, C falling cohort size, I affected ground items, R fungal roots/path entries, G grates, B bulwarks, S serialized non-COW run data, and E scheduled environment catch-up steps.

- Stair departure builds one exact distance field per eligible ally. Each requires O(V*(G+B+hazard lookup)+V log V) worst-case time with current vector membership and heap pathfield helpers, O(V) scratch. Queue/arrival scans and actor lookup add O(F*A); queue sort is O(F log F). Return routes include the same pathfield cost per returning follower. Fields are not shared across actor-specific habitats/effects.
- Arrival occupancy is O(A) plus9 fixed candidate checks. Vertical ally landing checks bounded radius rings, with actor-aware passability/hazards; no unbounded random placement attempts.
- Forced fall sorts candidate IDs O(C log C), builds occupancy O(A), and reserves each landing with a complete row-major O(V) scan and actor-aware eligibility. Each actor transfer can scan O(A) and run fire/movement/damage hooks. Depth views clone COW collections but also O(S) scalar/string collections; actual mutation may detach O(V+floor metadata) or O(A_depth) blocks. Visibility/razorleaf refresh and damage hooks are additional subsystem costs.
- Collapsing terrain considers a radius2 disk (at most25 cells), scans source actors/items O(A+I), then authoritative placements and fall cohorts. Root severing scans O(R) across floors. Each item/corpse placement may scan up to D floor cell/occupancy sets; O(sum(V_d+A_d)) for corpse placement plus O(R) severing. Normalization scans actors, sorts D depths and invokes placement for displaced corpses.
- Any floor transition can generate missing floors and run E catch-up steps; those generation/environment costs are additive and can dominate the local algorithms. Observation, event/history serialization and normal engine phases are additional costs documented by their owners.

These are conservative analytical bounds, not benchmark results. Profile complete generated runs, large follower cohorts, occupied landing maps and expired-flight cohorts before optimization claims.
