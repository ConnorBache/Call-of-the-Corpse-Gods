# Native bestiary: solid color and Natural History

- One entry per monster, ordered by first appearance; companion-only entries last.
- Solid parchment-to-red-to-black full-screen colors, darkening monotonically by depth.
- Inconsolata Regular and ExtraBold throughout the bestiary, bundled in the executable.
- Fixed main layout regions, individual illustration scale/lift and title treatments.
- Bastidion, Blorb and Cave Bat contain exact owner-authored Natural History. The other 78 entries retain Latin placeholders until supplied individually.
- No invented lore or creature/mechanical changes.

The current source was recovered from persistent source revision5, then the continuous roster and explicit art-direction recipes from the completed morning revision were reconstructed with the original authors. Environmental scenery is intentionally superseded by the latest solid-background request. This revision must be saved as a new version of the persistent source archive alongside the Windows build.

## History-only release, 2026-10-21

The clean-art baseline is restored. Only Bastidion, Blorb and Cave Bat Natural History text changes remain. All recent per-page overlays, callouts, annotations and custom column splits are removed. Existing 81-entry order, solid backgrounds, Inconsolata typography, artwork and mechanics remain unchanged.

The metadata paint-order correction is retained: family/first-appearance text is painted after the title mask instead of being erased by it. No positions, typography or styles change.
